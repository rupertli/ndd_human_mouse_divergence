Data and interpretation

This page uses the NDD expression/K-means v2 results. Cluster membership comes from the supplied per-cluster gene lists. All exported HPO, GO, and KEGG rows for the selected cluster are shown, without selected-gene filtering or new enrichment tests. The selected gene is highlighted in the member list.

The expression chart uses the exported cluster means and standard errors across samples in each developmental phase. Shading is mean plus/minus one SE, not a confidence interval. Phases are equally spaced ordered categories, not proportional elapsed time; these bulk developmental categories are distinct from the five cortical event bins used for Orphanet context below.

GO and KEGG overlap lists use Entrez IDs, matched to gene symbols through a bundled mapping generated locally from org.Hs.eg.db; unmapped IDs remain explicitly labeled as Entrez IDs. HPO uses gene symbols; community labels are shown only when supplied. GO ontology labels are BP (biological process), CC (cellular component), and MF (molecular function).

Header-only files are empty results, not load errors. The supplied NDD HPO exports cover clusters 4 and 11 only; other clusters are labeled unavailable. Neither condition establishes absence of annotations. GO and KEGG may use different backgrounds from HPO; adjusted p-values should not be compared as if they were the same analysis. Disease pathways are annotation categories, not diagnoses.

Orphanet disease-onset context: exact HPO associations from Orphadata phenotypes are joined by ORPHAcode to natural history, using the matching 23 June 2026 XML exports. Associations with frequency "Excluded (0%)" are omitted. Disease groups and subtypes are retained, with one count per unique clinical entity per onset category. This is a database distribution, not patient incidence; related subtypes are not independent diseases. The join is not restricted to ACTB-associated diseases and cannot identify an HPO term's biological onset, diagnostic age, or most likely phase.

Age conversion assumptions: Orphadata definitions, Table 8, page 32 specify antenatal (before birth), neonatal (birth to week 4), infancy (end of week 4 through month 23), childhood (2-11 years), adolescent (12-18), adult (19-65), elderly (after 65), and all ages (no peak). For interval overlap only, birth is assumed at 266 postconception days (38 weeks after conception), a year is 365.25 days, and completed-year ranges end at the next birthday. "All ages" is conservatively open-ended; unknown onset is never imputed. Gestational variability can change boundary overlap.

Five-bin alignment: human boundaries are 49.95, 87.27, 152.47, 266.40, 465.45, and 979.46 postconception days from the cortical alignment file. Intervals are half-open: antenatal overlaps bins 1-3 and earlier ages; neonatal overlaps bins 3-4 (bin 3 covers only the first 0.40 postnatal day under this birth assumption); infancy overlaps bins 4-5 and later ages; childhood and older categories fall after bin 5. These are exploratory cortical alignment bins, not clinical onset phases or evidence that cortical expression caused a phenotype.
